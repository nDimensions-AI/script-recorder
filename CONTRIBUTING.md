nDimensions.AI Script Recorder is maintained by nDimensions.AI and CharanTheAiGuy.
Discuss substantial changes with the maintainers before implementation.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to provide it
under this project's [MIT License](LICENSE). Existing upstream and third-party
material retains its original copyright, license, and attribution. Do not
replace those notices or submit material whose redistribution terms are unknown.

Target the branch agreed with the maintainers in this project's own repository.
Run checks appropriate to your change and describe the behavior and validation.
Follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). This fork does not use Microsoft's
contributor agreement or contribution channels.
